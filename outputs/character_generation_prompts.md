# Character Generation Prompts

These prompts are split into two layers:
- `Base appearance`: core look and vibe of the adult character.
- `Shot format`: reusable instruction for the product-card framing.

## Reusable Shot Format

Use this add-on with every generation:

`полный рост, персонаж стоит в кадре целиком с головы до пят, centered composition, clean readable silhouette, fashion product card style, realistic lighting, clean background without clutter`
